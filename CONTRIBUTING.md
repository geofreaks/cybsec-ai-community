# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve CybSec-AI during beta.

## Best Contributions

- Clear bug reports with the exact ask, expected behavior, and actual behavior.
- Cyber security questions that should be understood but were not.
- Product-specific how-to gaps with official documentation links.
- Upload-analysis scenarios with synthetic or sanitized examples.
- Training resources that are reputable, current, and defensive.
- UI/UX feedback with screenshots and screen size details.

## Safety Rules

Do not submit:

- Secrets, passwords, tokens, private keys, cookies, or session data.
- Private incident evidence or customer logs.
- Raw malware or exploit payloads.
- Instructions for unauthorized scanning, exploitation, phishing, evasion, or credential theft.
- Data copied from private, leaked, or paid sources without permission.

## How To File A Useful Issue

Include:

- The exact ask or workflow.
- What CybSec-AI answered or did.
- What you expected instead.
- Why it matters for defensive cyber security work.
- Any official public references that should guide the answer.

If the report involves a real organization, redact identifying details unless you are authorized to share them publicly.
